About

Grounded in real problems, not just coursework

I'm a final-year BSc Information Technology student at the University of Ghana, about to begin national service. Over the past year I moved from classroom exercises to building software that real people depend on — most recently a school management system now used operationally by Eagle Vision Basic School in Accra.

I care about the parts of a system that don't show up in a screenshot: validated input, parameterized queries, sessions that lock out after repeated failed logins. Interfaces should be easy for the people using them, and the code behind them should be hard to break.

Going into national service, I'm looking to bring that same care to a team working on web or software development — somewhere I can keep learning while shipping things that matter.
